# Where the sounds come from

| File | Played | Made from |
|---|---|---|
| `opening/spoolup.wav` | when the countdown starts | a fleet carrier's spool-up |
| `cruising/ed-roar.wav`, `ed-roar.far.wav` | while the cloud is open (a 10 s loop, near and far) | the roar of a capital ship's jump cloud, with the strikes taken out |
| `prejump/spoolup.wav` | in the last seconds before the jump | the top of the carrier's spool-up, into its suck-out |
| `jump/ed-burst.wav` | at the jump | the burst and rumble of a capital ship's jump |
| `closing/ed-dissipate.wav` | as the cloud fades | the capital ship's roar falling away |
| `abort/tear-discharge.wav` | when a jump is aborted | the capital ship's burst and the carrier's strikes, over the carrier's power-down |

All of them are edited from the audio of two Elite Dangerous videos on YouTube, both made by other people:

- a capital ship's jump: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f-OTVKg2xI0
- a Victory class fleet carrier's jump: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jthw7BW5WOY

Only the capital ship and the carrier were used, never the player's own ship.

These sounds come from another game. Whether the plugin may ship them has not been decided yet, and that has to be
settled before the plugin is published.

The lightning is not in this folder. The plugin cuts it from Space Engineers' own weather lightning
(`Content/Audio/ARC/EVENT/Lightning_0.wav` and `Lightning_2.wav`) on the player's machine and keeps the cuts in
`Storage/BlackSmoke/Lightning/se` in the game's user data folder.
